# Mode and Routing Policy

## Automatic Modes

| Time remaining | Mode | Mastery work | Exam work |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| More than 30 days | mastery | 80% | 20% |
| 8–30 days | balanced | 60% | 40% |
| 7 days or fewer | exam | 20% | 80% |

No exam date means mastery mode unless the learner explicitly chooses otherwise.

## Priority Order

1. Repair a critical prerequisite failure.
2. Handle overdue reviews for high-weight concepts.
3. Practice the weakest concept with independent output.
4. Add new breadth.
5. Add transfer or authentic performance tasks.
6. Run exam-condition checks.

Repeated critical errors outrank new breadth even in exam mode. Near the exam, reduce coverage expansion and protect already fragile high-yield concepts.

## Weekly Planning

- Reserve the first block for due retrieval, not rereading.
- Use one block for new concepts and one block for mixed practice.
- End the week with a short diagnostic or timed section.
- Keep at least one recovery block for mistakes that recur.
- Reduce the plan when available time is below the estimated workload rather than hiding the gap.

## Skill Acquisition

Replace examination tasks with a real deliverable, performance rubric, or authentic task. Track demonstrations, feedback, and transfer instead of percentage scores. A skill can be mastered when the learner performs the target behavior independently in a new context.
