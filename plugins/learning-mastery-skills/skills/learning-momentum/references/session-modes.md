# Session Modes

| Mode | Time | Output | Use when |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Comeback | 5-10 min | one retrieval and one next question | returning, low time, low energy |
| Steady | 20-30 min | one recalled idea plus one independent task | normal weekday |
| Deep | 45-75 min | Feynman Loop plus transfer | high energy, difficult concept |
| Explore | 10-30 min | one connection or experiment | curiosity is high, score pressure is low |
| Repair | 15-40 min | one prerequisite fixed and retested | repeated or critical failure |

## Comeback Script

1. Ask one question from a recent success.
2. Ask one question from a current weak point.
3. Choose the next 10-minute action.
4. Stop at the promised boundary unless the learner wants to continue.

The purpose is re-entry, not maximum coverage.

## Deep Session Script

1. Retrieve prerequisites.
2. Surface one precise confusion.
3. Run a full Feynman cycle.
4. Transfer to a new context.
5. Leave one open question for the next session.
